# Paper Constellations

Six interactive 3D sketches of maps around a research paper, drawn for the
[Reader](https://github.com/saurav717/reader) app: what a paper stands on and
what stands on it, who its authors work with, what it sits beside, where it
sits in its field, which section leans on which reference, and what you have
already read of all that.

Open `index.html` in a browser. Drag a map to turn it, hover a point for its
paper. No build step and no dependencies beyond two Google Fonts; the 3D is a
small projection drawn on a 2D canvas, so it runs anywhere.

The example data is a stand-in built around *Attention Is All You Need* so the
shapes are recognisable. None of the numbers are real.

The page ends with a comparison of the six ideas (data needed, calls per
paper, whether 3D earns its keep) and a sketch of where a **Map** tab would
live in Reader's dock.
